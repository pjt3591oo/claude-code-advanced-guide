# Feedback scope

Choose feedback from what changed, who can resolve it and how long the information remains useful. Severity and placement are separate decisions: a serious error in one operation need not block unrelated work.

Keep waiting beside its owner. A pending action can retain its label and footprint while acknowledging input; a missing content region can reserve its expected structure. Block the whole surface only when the work actually depends on that operation. Show measurable progress only when it is known. Replacing already useful content with a fresh skeleton can erase orientation during a small update.

Locate a correctable input error beside the answer and preserve what was entered. A service failure, denied capability or unavailable item needs its actual next step; styling it as an invalid field asks the person to repair the wrong cause. Validate when there is enough input to judge. Earlier guidance helps when it prevents wasted effort, but incomplete typing is not automatically an error. Temporary unavailability needs an understandable prerequisite; readable fixed information should not become illegible disabled text.

Let lifetime follow need. Brief acknowledgement may disappear when the result remains evident. Time spent in a hidden page or using the message's action is not necessarily reading time; pause expiry or retain another way to find the outcome when needed. Changing message lifetime does not extend a real deadline on its action. An unresolved failure or essential recovery action needs a durable home. Reserve interruption for a decision that truly blocks continuation. Announce relevant changes without moving focus unnecessarily; repeated announcements can also obstruct work.

Trace one pending action through success, failure and retry. The person should know what happened, what remains intact and what they can do next. Repeated attempts must not look like unrelated new tasks.

Sound or vibration can reinforce an event; its essential meaning must remain inspectable when either channel is absent. Respect preferences and available hardware rather than inventing stronger substitute effects. Repeat the task with optional cues unavailable and check whether the outcome and next action remain understandable.
