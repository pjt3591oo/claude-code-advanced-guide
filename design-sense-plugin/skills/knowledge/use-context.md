# Conditions of use

A usable arrangement fits the person doing the operation under its actual conditions. The same person may have a hand occupied, reduced dexterity, divided attention or no private place to listen. Age, device category and screen width do not establish those conditions. Identify a constraint that changes a decision rather than inventing a complete persona.

Reach depends on hand, grip, device and posture. Reaching a control once does not establish comfort through repeated use. Follow the whole operation: reach, activate, read the result and correct a miss. Look for stretching, regripping, finger occlusion and accidental adjacent actions. A reachable equivalent may help; moving every action to the bottom can separate it from its object or obstruct editing. A mounted display presents different constraints from a handheld one. Resolve target boundaries through [action ownership](action-ownership.md).

Viewing distance, lighting and movement can change what remains legible or distinguishable. Inspect the decisive value and state under the relevant condition before adding more emphasis everywhere. Prefer a clear reading sequence and distinguishable information to decorative detail that survives only at close inspection. A simulated small viewport cannot establish outdoor readability or comfort in motion.

When attention is interrupted, remembered context becomes unreliable. Keep information needed for the next decision available with that decision. Use [interaction continuity](interaction-continuity.md) to restore orientation; preserving data alone does not restore understanding. A short uninterrupted expert task may need less persistent guidance than an occasional task resumed after a delay.

Treat an assumed use condition as a hypothesis. Observe relevant people performing a neutral task goal with their ordinary equipment when possible; avoid coaching the path and then claiming discoverability. A self-check can expose a failure, but cannot establish comfort for other people. Record what changed the design and which conditions remain unobserved.
