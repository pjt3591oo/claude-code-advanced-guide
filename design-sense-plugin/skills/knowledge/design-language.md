# Art direction

Give the product character through the work it presents. A visual editor can feel precise through compact tool rhythm and exact alignment; an editorial story can feel expansive through image sequence, type and reading pace. Quietness suits some tasks. Expressiveness suits others.

Translate adjectives into relationships. “Confident” might mean a strong typographic opening and restrained supporting elements; it does not prescribe a font or a blue button. Inspect a reference in a comparable state and ask what creates the effect: proportion, density, image treatment, contrast, repetition or movement. A palette alone leaves those decisions unresolved. A source can explain domain rules without being a useful composition reference. Match visual evidence to the decision, information demand and state, even when the subject differs.

Carry one coherent direction across a representative screen and a contrasting state. Choose typography using actual labels, numerals and the product's writing system. Choose imagery for what it lets people understand or feel. Large type or a distinctive asset earns space when it carries the subject; chrome should support that choice.

When the direction remains uncertain, compare alternatives in the same task and surrounding screen. Vary the relationship in question, such as hierarchy, density or interaction, while keeping realistic content comparable. A palette swap cannot answer a structural question; a detached component may conceal its cost to neighboring content.

Let expression change with the task. An editorial introduction can set a mood; a request form should name the action and outcome directly. Carry the same identity through type, spacing and state treatment. Persistent navigation earns its content through orientation or action.

If a design feels generic, locate the interchangeable decision. Replacing the logo should not be enough to turn a reading experience into a work queue. Change content order, scale or rhythm before adding ornament. Preserve intentional brand choices even when they differ from these examples.
